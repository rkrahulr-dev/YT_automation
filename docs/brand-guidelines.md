# AI Play Hub brand guidelines

The full page is the AI Play Hub Brand Guidelines design system on claude.ai. Tokens are in `brand/tokens.json`. The channel name is still open: where this says AI Play Hub, swap the final name in. A fan page for GTA 6 clips, settings, tips and news, not made or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

## Content fundamentals

- Short plain sentences in second person. Lead with the specific fact or moment, never "Hey guys".
- Cover lines are six words or fewer, in capitals. No emoji in slides or captions; one emoji at most in the bio.
- Every clip credits the streamer by name and platform: "Clip: <streamer> on <platform>, credited with permission." Use only once permission is confirmed.
- Facts only when checked against the game or a named source, and name the source in the caption when it comes from a guide.
- Three hashtags per post: #GTA6, #GTAVI and one topic tag. One ask per post.

| pink | #ff3d8b | #c7125e | Hook words, main accent |
| orange | #ff8a3d | #b8480a | Highlights, numbers, sun motif |
| cyan | #2ad4d0 | #08766f | Links, tags, handle |
| on-accent | #160f26 | #ffffff | Text on an accent fill |

- Fonts (free on Google Fonts): Unbounded for headlines, DM Sans for body text, JetBrains Mono for small labels.
- Carousel slides are 1080x1350 with a 120px safe margin. Cover line 120px, slide headline 72px, body 44px, handle and slide number 32px. Number every slide and put the handle in the bottom corner.
- Reels and stories are 1080x1920. Keep text between y=250 and y=1600, inside the middle of the frame.
- Corners use radius 20px on color blocks and cards, pill radius on the profile photo circle.
- The sun motif is an orange disc cut by horizontal slits, used once per post as a background shape, never behind text. No gradients, drop shadows or blur on slides.

## Iconography

No logo yet. Set the name in Unbounded until one exists. Use a simple controller glyph or the sun disc as the only motifs. Never use Rockstar or Take-Two logos, GTA lettering, game art or screenshots as brand elements.
