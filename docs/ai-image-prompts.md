# AI image prompt kit

Paste a Master block, then one Look block, then the Negative block, into the image tool (ChatGPT image, Midjourney or any other). Replace text in [brackets]. All images are original: see the rules in `docs/brand-guidelines.md`.

## Master block (start every prompt with this)

Stylized Vice City inspired Florida coastal city at sunset, saturated hot pink, magenta and violet sky with an orange glow at the horizon, turquoise water, tall palm trees, pastel Art Deco buildings with neon signs, cinematic lighting, rich contrast, crisp detail, original design, entirely invented characters and places.

## Negative block (end every prompt with this)

No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no Grand Theft Auto or Rockstar branding, no game interface, no real people or celebrity likeness, no brand names, no readable license plates, no distorted hands.

## Look blocks

### 1. Aerial golden hour (4:5 for covers, 16:9 for thumbnails)

High angle drone view of a long cable-stayed bridge over a sparkling bay, [heavy traffic of colorful cars | a red supercar escaping a line of police cars], two news helicopters with searchlights, white yachts and a marina, a low sun breaking through dramatic clouds, palm-lined coast and a glass skyline. Keep the top 40% as open sky.

### 2. Neon dusk street (9:16 for stories and reel cards, 4:5 for covers)

Wet seaside boulevard at twilight, a tall palm tree framing the left edge, a vertical pink neon sign on the right, a lone retro pink sports car with glowing tail lights, glossy asphalt reflecting a magenta and violet sky, a lit skyline across the water, flocks of birds. [Optional: an invented couple seen from behind, sitting on the car trunk, looking at the skyline.] Leave the top third as open sky and keep the lower third darker and quiet.

### 3. Painted ensemble (4:5)

Painterly illustrated group poster of six invented adult characters in confident poses: a silver-haired man in a pink linen blazer holding a drink, a tattooed man in a black tank top and gold chain, a woman officer in a dark vest, a man in a palm-print shirt, a woman in tinted sunglasses and a pink outfit, a man in a purple shirt with braids. Pastel pink and teal Art Deco hotels, palms and a pink supercar behind them, the artwork fading out to clean white at the bottom. Everyday clothing, no swimwear.

### 4. Leonida day street (4:5 or 9:16)

Realistic third-person view of a muscular man in a black tank top and cargo shorts, seen from behind, walking down a sunny cracked sidewalk toward a two-storey red-pink motel, a classic muscle car parked outside, utility poles and tangled wires, a white water tower, palms in the distance, bright blue sky with scattered clouds, hard midday shadows, gritty everyday atmosphere.

### 5. Flat Deco sunset (1:1 for profile photo, 9:16 for story backgrounds)

Flat vector illustration, purple to pink to orange sunset gradient sky, two tall palm tree silhouettes in muted violet, pastel Art Deco buildings in lavender, peach and cream with soft gradients, three gulls, clean shapes, no outlines, minimal detail, large calm sky area.

## Checking an image before it is used

1. No logo, wordmark or readable text anywhere, even faint.
2. Characters are adults, everyday outfits, faces small.
3. Colors sit in the brand palette: violet, pink, orange, gold, turquoise.
4. The top 40% (covers) or lower third (stories) is quiet enough for text.
5. The post says AI-generated, using Instagram's AI label, and the image is not presented as gameplay.
